RAG∞ Ultimate — User Manual

How to use the app every day and how to test it, for the three kinds of people who use it: Admin runs the system, Loader adds the documents, User asks questions.

Everyone should read sections 1–7 (15 minutes). Then read your own role's guide and do its tests. Use the buttons at the top-left to show only your parts.

Your app's address Hugging Face: https://huggingface.co/spaces/MVSPC16/rag-ultimate
Streamlit: https://<your-app-name>.streamlit.app
Your admin will tell you which one to use, plus your username and first password.

1. Welcome — what the app does

RAG∞ Ultimate is a question-and-answer assistant for your organisation's documents: datasheets, manuals, reports, spreadsheets, presentations, scanned pages and pictures.

🔎 It looks things up

When you ask a question, it searches every document you are allowed to see and picks the passages that answer it.

📎 It shows its sources

Every fact in an answer has a reference such as [REF-2: TABLE p3] = evidence 2, a table on page 3. You can open and read the evidence.

✓ It checks numbers

Numbers are compared with the source before you see them. Checked answers show ✓ Verified.

🚫 It does not guess

If the documents do not contain the answer it says NOT FOUND IN DOCUMENT instead of inventing one.

⚡ It remembers good answers

The same question about the same documents is answered instantly from the cache.

🔒 It keeps data apart

You only see the documents your role is allowed to see; your chats are private to you.
Always rememberThe answer is only as good as the documents. If a document is old, wrong or missing, the answer will be too. When something matters (safety, money, contracts), open the evidence and check it.

2. The three roles

In the app the roles are called admin, editor and viewer. In this manual:

Admin (app role: admin)

Sets up and looks after the system: creates accounts, decides who can do what, sets the safety rules, watches quality, usage and cost. Can do everything a Loader and a User can.

Loader (app role: editor)

Brings knowledge in: uploads documents, labels them, keeps them up to date, controls who may read them, removes old ones, and checks the answers are right. Can also ask questions.

User (app role: viewer)

Asks questions and uses the answers in their work. Gives 👍/👎 feedback. Cannot upload or change documents or settings.

3. Signing in & your account

  1. Open the app addressUse Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a computer or phone. If you see “Sleeping” or “Building”, wait about a minute — the app is waking up.
  2. Type your username and password → Sign inUsernames are lower-case. Passwords are case-sensitive.
  3. Change your first passwordLeft side bar → 🔑 Change my password → current password, new password twice → Change password. Use 10+ characters with letters and digits.
  4. Sign out when you finishSide bar → Sign out, especially on shared computers.
Locked out?After 5 wrong passwords the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask the Admin to use Reset a password / unlock an account.
Forgot your password?Ask the Admin. They set a temporary password and tell you privately; then change it yourself.

Under each answer: Evidence (the exact passages), How this answer was made (plain-English steps), 📋 How this answer was produced (summary) (documents searched, evidence found, model used), 👍/👎, Copy and ↻ Regenerate.

6. Asking good questions

✅ Be specific

“What is the maximum input voltage of the LM7805X?”
beats “voltage?”

✅ Name the thing

Use part numbers, model names, section names, units and conditions (“at 25 °C”).

✅ One question at a time

Then follow up: “And at 85 °C?” — the chat remembers the topic.

✅ Ask for a format

“List the pins as a table”, “Give the steps in order”.

✅ Ask for calculations

“What is the power dissipation at 12 V in and 1 A?” — shows the formula and sources.

✅ New topic = New chat

Keeps follow-ups from mixing two subjects.
AvoidQuestions about things not in the documents (company history, opinions, today's prices), pasting passwords or personal data, and instructions aimed at the AI itself (“ignore your rules…”) — those are blocked.

7. Getting help

  • ❔ Help button (top-right of every page): type a question such as how do I upload; suggested questions match the page you are on.
  • “What is this page?” panels at the top of pages (turn on/off in Settings).
  • 🎓 Guide & Test Lab → ⑤ Help center: the full help.
  • Still stuck: tell your Loader (document problems) or Admin (access, errors). Include the time, your question and a screenshot.

User 8. User guide

Your job: find reliable answers fast and tell the team when something is wrong.

Your daily routine

  1. Sign in → ChatCheck Answering from lists the documents you need. If one is missing, tell the Loader.
  2. Ask your questionSpecific, one at a time (section 6). Use New chat for a new topic.
  3. Check before you use itLook for ✓ Verified. For anything important, open Evidence and read the page.
  4. Narrow the search when neededChat filters: document category, tags, element types (e.g. only tables) → Apply; Clear to undo.
  5. Give feedback👍 good answer · 👎 wrong/unhelpful (write a short reason) → Send feedback. This improves the answer style and alerts the team.
  6. Reuse answersCopy into your e-mail or report; keep the reference ([REF…]) so others can check.
  7. Find old conversationsSide bar → Recent chats.

You can also

  • Documents: see and search the documents you are allowed to read (title, tag, part number, parameter).
  • Settings → Answer style: shorter or more detailed answers, bullet points, etc.
  • ⋮ (top-right) → Settings → Light / Dark theme.
What you cannot do (by design)Upload or delete documents, change other people's access, or see other users' chats. Ask your Loader or Admin.

Loader 9. Loader guide

Your job: put the right documents in, keep them current and labelled, control who can read them, and check the answers are correct.

9.1 Before you upload — prepare the files

  • Accepted: PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, CSV, text, Markdown, HTML, JSON, images (JPG/PNG). Max size per file: 50 MB (unless your Admin changed it).
  • Prefer the original digital file over a scan. Scans and photos work (OCR reads them) but are less accurate.
  • Give files clear, stable names (e.g. LM7805X_datasheet.pdf). Uploading a file with the same name later makes it the new version.
  • Remove passwords from PDFs. Programs (.exe etc.) are rejected even if renamed.

9.2 Upload documents

  1. Documents → drop files on “Drop documents here”You can drop several at once.
  2. Choose the Sensitivity
    SensitivityUse forEffect
    publicPublished datasheets, brochuresNormal
    internal (default)Normal company documentsNormal
    confidentialCustomer data, pricing, unreleased designsOnly local AI models may read it; on the online app (no local models) it is not sent to cloud AI
    restrictedHighest secrecySame, plus limit the roles that may read it (9.4)
  3. Wait for the green message“name is ready”, “updated to version 2” or “was already indexed” (a duplicate, nothing added).
  4. Many or large files?Use Ingestion → background upload and keep working; press ↻ Refresh to follow the jobs. Failed jobs say why.

9.3 Label documents (metadata)

Open a document card → 🏷️ Metadata. The app suggests title, author, category, tags, part numbers and parameters (with units). Each value shows its source (auto / you) and confidence.

  1. Check the suggestionsCorrect anything wrong, especially Category, Tags and Part numbers.
  2. Save“Metadata saved. Filters and search use it immediately.” Users can now filter Chat by category/tags and search by part number.
Agree on labelsUse the same category names and tags for the whole team (e.g. Datasheet, Manual, Test report). Consistent labels = better filters.

9.4 Control who can read a document

Documents → Access control & versions → pick the document → Sensitivity and Readable by roles (empty = everyone in your organisation) → Update permissions. You can change your own documents; Admins can change all.

9.5 Keep documents current

  • New revision: upload with the same file name → “updated to version N”. The old version is no longer searched; history stays under Version history.
  • Temporarily hide: switch a document off on its card; Chat stops using it.
  • Remove for good: Delete a document → choose → Delete permanently (all versions). Cannot be undone.

9.6 Check that answers are right

  1. Ask 3–5 questions you know the answers toin Chat, right after uploading.
  2. Validate → choose the document → ✨ Generate test questions → runRead the score; open failed questions and their evidence. 💾 Save this question set to re-run after the next revision.
  3. If an answer is wrongRetrieval debugger: type the question and see which passages were found. Typical fixes: upload a cleaner file, correct metadata, split a very large document.
  4. Follow up on 👎Check Analytics → Feedback weekly (or ask the Admin) for questions users marked wrong.

Admin 10. Admin guide

Your job: give people the right access, keep it safe and running, and watch quality and usage.

10.1 First day — set up the team

  1. Sign in with the admin account and change its passwordSide bar → 🔑 Change my password. (Changing ADMIN_PASSWORD in the server settings later does not change an existing admin's password.)
  2. Guide & Test Lab → ① Start hereFix any red item; yellow ones are optional.
  3. Users & roles → Create userfor each person: username (lower-case, e.g. asha.k), initial password, role (viewer = User, editor = Loader, admin = Admin). Give the password privately; ask them to change it.
  4. Keep admins fewTwo admins is enough (one backup). Everyone else viewer or editor.
  5. Agree the labelswith your Loaders (categories, tags, sensitivity rules).

10.3 Safety rules (🛡️ Security)

  • block stops a request; warn lets it through but records it.
  • Restricted topics: one per line, synonyms with | (e.g. salary|compensation).
  • Personal data handling and usage limits.
  • Test a prompt: see what would happen to any text without asking the AI. Use it before saving a new rule.

10.4 AI models (🤖 Models · 🔀 Model routing)

  • Text answers: Groq main model → 3 Groq fallback models → a free OpenRouter model. Images: free OpenRouter vision models, in order. Backup API keys are tried before switching model.
  • Test connection (no cost) and Test vision model (one tiny image) after any key change.
  • Key pool table shows keys only as “key #1, #2…” with their state (ready / cooling down / rejected).
  • Usage: calls, failures, tokens, cost per model. Free models cost $0; a $0.01/day guard blocks paid ones.
  • Changing keys: in the hosting settings (Streamlit Secrets / Hugging Face Variables and secrets) — the app restarts.

10.5 Answer cache & help (⚡ Cache & help)

  • Hit rate and saved tokens show how much repeated questions save.
  • 🗑 Clear cached answers of my organisation after big document changes or if an old answer must disappear immediately (new versions already clear their own cache).
  • ❔ Help questions: add your own Q&A (e.g. “Who do I contact for access?”); they appear in the ❔ Help panel.
  • 🏷️ Metadata overview: categories and parts across all documents.

13. Acceptance test plan

Do this once the app is live, before the team relies on it, and again after big changes. Each person tests their own role; then all three do the team tests together. About 60–90 minutes in total.

  1. Admin prepares(Admin tests A1–A4): creates test accounts test.user (viewer) and test.loader (editor), and loads the sample documents (Guide & Test Lab → ③ Test Lab → 📥 Load sample documents).
  2. Each role runs its testsTick each one that passes. If one fails: note the ID, time, a screenshot (no passwords), and continue.
  3. Team tests(13d) together, then fill the sign-off sheet (14).

15. Do's & don'ts

✅ Do

  • Check the evidence for important answers
  • Give 👍/👎 feedback
  • Keep document names stable so new versions replace old ones
  • Mark confidential documents as confidential
  • Sign out on shared computers
  • Report wrong answers with the question and time

❌ Don't

  • Share your password or account
  • Paste passwords, card numbers or personal data into questions
  • Upload documents you are not allowed to share inside the organisation
  • Use an answer without a reference for decisions
  • Keep obsolete documents active
  • Make everyone an admin

17. Words used

Reference [REF-n: TYPE pX]Points to evidence n, its type (text, table, figure) and page.
VerifiedNumbers in the answer were found in the evidence.
CachedRe-used from an earlier identical question with the same documents and settings.
MetadataLabels about a document: title, category, tags, part numbers, parameters.

VersionA newer upload with the same file name replaces the old one in searches.
IngestionReading and indexing an uploaded document so it can be searched.
Fallback model / backup keyWhat the system tries automatically if the main AI model or key is busy or failing.
Audit logPermanent record of sign-ins, uploads, deletions and rule changes.
